Account and Data Deletion - MatchDay

Effective date: November 10, 2026

At MatchDay, we respect your privacy and give you control over your personal data. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from our servers (hosted via Supabase), you can follow the steps below.

How to Request Account Deletion

Method 1: Direct Request via Email (Fastest)

Send an email to our support team at support@picoteapps.com (or your contact email) using the email address registered with your MatchDay account. Please use the subject line: "Request for Account Deletion - MatchDay". We will process and wipe your data within 7 business days.

Method 2: In-App Request

Log in to the MatchDay application, navigate to your Profile / Settings menu, and select the option to delete your account. This will instantly remove your authentication profile and associated local data.

+

Types of Data Deleted

Once your deletion request is confirmed and processed, the following information is permanently erased:

  • Your account credentials (email and user ID)
  • Profile details (first name, last name, and avatars)
  • Match statistics, ratings, and player associations linked to your account

Data Retention

Backup copies containing anonymized system logs may be retained for up to 30 days for security and integrity purposes before being permanently purged.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding data deletion, feel free to reach out through our official GitHub repository or support channels.
